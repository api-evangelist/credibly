Merchant cash advances (MCAs) and working capital loans offer short-term capital for your business. An MCA is a purchase of your future receivables in exchange for an upfront lump sum, with fixed daily or weekly remittances. A working capital loan provides funds to help run your business, with a clear repayment structure and timeline.
